# KLSCM Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis V1 Methodology

## Scope and research design

Topic discovery precedes ABSA because the frozen c1 taxonomy establishes the substantive discourse population. Topics describe recurring themes; aspects identify the evaluated target inside a caption. They are therefore linked for context and aggregation but are not interchangeable labels. ABSA v1 is restricted to the 7,704 records in `final_substantive_topic_corpus_v1.csv`. The 5,703 unassigned outliers and 336 contextual/excluded-topic records remain preserved and are not characterized as irrelevant.

ABSA v1 analyzes original multilingual captions directly and does not translate the corpus as preprocessing. English glosses are auxiliary. Language metadata never excludes a record. Topic name/group may be supplied as prompt context, and this fact is recorded, but the prompt prohibits topic-to-aspect leakage.

## Unit of analysis and ontology

The unit is an evidence-grounded aspect mention. A document may yield zero, one, or many mentions, and different aspects may have different sentiments. V1 deliberately analyzes evaluative mentions only: factual aspect references yield zero mentions. `neutral` is retained only for an explicitly non-polar evaluation, not an unevaluated fact. `mixed` requires both polarities toward the same aspect in the same local context.

The frozen ontology contains 20 families, including `emerging_other`. Its definitions and boundaries are in `ABSA_ASPECT_ONTOLOGY_V1.md` and the versioned JSON authority. Every mention preserves document/source/year/language, raw c1 topic, final topic ID/name/group, target, sentiment, confidence, exact evidence and offsets, explicit/implicit type, auxiliary gloss, meaningful hashtags/emoji, and rationale. IDs are deterministic. Exact-substring validation prevents ungrounded evidence.

## Blinded single-researcher validation and 80-document supersession

The original 150-document plan was generated with seed 20260819 but was superseded before annotation or scoring to reduce the burden on one researcher. Its CSV and manifest remain immutable historical evidence; `absa_validation_sample_v1_supersession.json` records their status and hashes. The current protocol is `absa_v1_single_researcher_audit_v1`.

The current sample contains 80 documents selected with distinct seed 20260823: exactly one random document for each of 32 final topic IDs, 24 difficult/multilingual enrichments, and 24 uniform random records from the remainder. Enrichment uses only observed language/mixed status, length, clause structure, emoji/hashtags, and first-person wording. No ABSA prediction influenced selection. The manifest reports topic, year, language, length quartile, source, first-person, and emoji/hashtag coverage. This supports broad qualitative error detection rather than population prevalence estimation.

The researcher records no evaluative mention or one/more rows with aspect, target, sentiment, exact evidence, explicit/implicit status, and an optional short note. Human confidence and long rationales are not required. The app validates evidence, autosaves, backs up, resumes, and locks completed documents against overwrite. A single annotator means inter-rater reliability is unavailable and no such claim is made.

Gold must be frozen before validation scoring. Freezing rejects incomplete work, invalid evidence/aspects/sentiments, misaligned IDs, and duplicates, and never overwrites versioned gold. Report aspect detection precision/recall/F1 using document+aspect matching, sentiment accuracy/macro-F1 and per-class metrics, joint metrics, exact evidence/span validity, zero-mention agreement, multi-aspect handling, and multilingual subgroup diagnostics where sample sizes permit.

Interpretation is predeclared but non-blocking. Strong means aspect F1 and sentiment macro-F1 at least 0.80 and grounding at least 0.95. Usable with limitations means aspect and sentiment metrics at least 0.70 and grounding at least 0.90. Materially lower results or systematic hallucination/grounding failures are weak. A slightly sub-0.80 result does not alone stop exploratory analysis; usable limitations are documented. Serious failure permits at most one controlled prompt/schema refinement, not an iterative v2/v3/v4 loop.

## Controlled precision-prompt development experiment (2026-08-19)

The diagnostic evidence motivated one and only one prompt candidate: `absa_v1_instructions_2_precision`. Its frozen hypothesis is that v1 detects evaluative language adequately but often fails local aspect-target attribution through adjacent-family assignment, unsupported expansion, contextual affect, duplication and sentiment spillover. The intervention retains the entire original instruction block and adds only general local-entailment, target attribution, temporal/epistemic, minimum-aspect and local-polarity policy plus a small synthetic contrast set. It does not encode row-specific answers.

The experiment reuses the same 80-document sample only as development evidence (`development_evidence=true`, `confirmatory_evidence=false`). Before prediction, its manifest froze the prompt hashes, all source hashes and the decision rule. Passing requires aspect precision >=0.55, aspect recall >=0.75, joint precision >0.3302752, joint F1 >=0.4528302, no more than seven gold-zero documents with candidate mentions, and qualitative confirmation that implicit/multilingual evaluation did not materially collapse. These gates cannot be changed by the finalizer.

The candidate retains `gpt-5.6-luna`, the frozen ontology, `absa_mention_schema_v1`, exact evidence recovery, preprocessing and the original document+aspect/stable-ID matching method. Its isolated cache stage is `absa_v1_mentions_precision_development_v1`. Exactly 80 requests were prepared locally; estimated Batch cost is USD 0.3045 under the existing planning rates. Paid submission has not occurred. If later imported, finalization will write only within the development directory and report baseline/candidate deltas, language and aspect-family metrics, baseline-FP transitions, reviewed-FP category behavior and baseline-TP recall regressions. Passing would mean eligibility for a future untouched confirmatory test, not validation on this reused sample.

## Execution, cost, and aggregation

### Residual diagnostic after the first precision candidate

The completed candidate development evaluation produced TP 77, FP 74 and FN 23, with aspect precision/recall/F1 0.5099/0.7700/0.6135. It improved substantially over the 84/134/16 baseline but did not reach the frozen 0.55 precision gate. This is reused-sample development evidence, not confirmatory validation.

An offline residual diagnostic applied the unchanged document+aspect/stable-ID matching logic, classified all baseline-prediction transitions, reviewed all 74 candidate FPs and analyzed all 23 candidate FNs. FPs fell 44.78%; gold-zero contamination improved from 11 to six documents and predicted multi-mention documents from 43 to 35. Residual researcher coding is dominated by ontology-boundary confusion (30), followed by context-as-evaluative (13), duplicate/redundant mention (12), aspect inference too far (nine) and over-decomposition (six). The original taxonomy remained sufficient.

Eleven candidate FNs were already baseline FNs and twelve are new, while five baseline FNs were recovered. The new misses include ontology shifts plus implicit, multilingual colloquial, comparative, hashtag/emoji and physical-experience signals. There is no aggregate multilingual collapse, but recall declined in English, Malay and the five-document Chinese group. The methodological conclusion is `FURTHER_PROMPT_INTERVENTION_JUSTIFIED` solely for a future controlled attribution/remapping intervention: single-proposition affect is still incorrectly owned by a separate emotional or adjacent family. Greater general conservatism is not supported because it would predictably worsen the observed recall costs. No additional prompt was created during this diagnostic.

### V3 aspect-ownership intervention prepared

One final candidate was justified by the residual concentration and prepared as `absa_v1_instructions_3_aspect_ownership`. It is a strict extension of V2 rather than a replacement: all V2 precision and recall safeguards remain, while the added policy reasons over evaluative propositions, assigns one primary owner by default, remaps genuine evaluation to the correct ontology family before suppressing it, and distinguishes semantic redundancy from legitimately separate propositions. Emotional and physical wording is treated as a separate aspect only when that state is itself the substantive target. Community-directed encouragement, comparative performance, embedded operational complaints, implicit/multilingual evaluation, hashtags/emoji and qualifying neutral judgments are explicitly retained.

The experiment `absa_v1_aspect_ownership_prompt_development_v1` reuses the frozen ordered 80-document development population and all previous success gates. Its pre-inference manifest hashes V2/V3 prompts, gold, ontology, sample, V2 predictions and the frozen 74-row residual review. Model, schema, request parameters, preprocessing, matching and evidence handling are unchanged; no code-level filtering or deduplication exists. Exactly 80 requests were generated locally with an estimated USD 0.3654 Batch cost. The paid Batch was not submitted. Finalization, if later authorized and completed, will compare V1/V2/V3 and specifically report emotional-experience FP, V2 ownership/duplicate residual transitions, V2-TP losses and gold/V1/V2/V3 multi-aspect counts. No V4 may be generated automatically regardless of outcome.
